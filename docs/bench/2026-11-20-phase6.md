# Bench note, Phase 6

2026-11-20, Windows 11, Intel Core Ultra 7 255H, MSVC (Visual Studio 18), the suites in Debug and
`likyu_bench` in Release. Phase 6 is the editor; what it measured is mostly what the editor does
not cost the audio thread, and the two changes it made below the editor: a band's channels, and
the analyser's tap.

## Timing

`likyu_bench time`, Release, 96 kHz, 64-sample blocks (666.7 µs), stereo, 6 s each, medians as a
share of one core.

| Scenario | Phase 6 | Phase 5 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 8 static bands | 0.435 % (2.9 µs) | 0.435 % |
| every ramp running (`moving`) | 1.26 % (8.4 µs) | 1.26 % |
| 8 dynamic bands on a stereo sidechain | 2.12 % (14.1 µs) | 2.22 % |
| 8 dynamic bands keyed by the input, unlinked | 3.51 % (23.4 µs) | 3.59 % |
| 8 Transient bands | 2.25 % (15.0 µs) | 2.25 % |
| 8 bands in Dynamic and Env on a stereo sidechain | 2.57 % (17.1 µs) | 2.66 % |
| **8 bands in Dynamic and Track** on a stereo sidechain: the PRD's budget case | **5.03 %** (33.5 µs) | 5.0 % |
| the same keyed by the input (`trackself`) | 4.80 % (32.0 µs) | 4.8 % |

- **A band's channels cost nothing measurable.** Every band's filter loop gained a test per channel
  (does this band apply to it?), and the bands keyed by the input are grouped by the channels they
  read. The figures are Phase 5's within the machine's own variation.
- **The analyser's tap** is not in these figures: the bench drives the DSP, and the tap is the
  processor's. Off, which is its default and every bench case, it costs one relaxed atomic load
  per block. On, it is a mean over the channels and two relaxed stores per sample; not timed.
- **The list of channels left out of the link**, retired this phase, took a branch out of the
  building of lanes, at grid points only: nothing to measure.

## The editor

Nothing of the editor runs on the audio thread. Its timer, 30 Hz, reads the 284 values and the
meter snapshot and repaints only what changed; with the analyser on, it also runs two 8192-point
FFTs a tick. Not timed: the Debug build draws at the default size, the analyser on, without a
visible stall.

| What | Measured |
| --- | --- |
| pluginval 1.0.4, strictness 10, Debug VST3, editor tests included (Editor, Open editor whilst processing, Editor Automation, Fuzz parameters) | passes, at every commit of the phase, on Windows; in CI on the three platforms |
| The editor under 48 configurations of random values of all 284 parameters, audio between (the validator's fuzzing, reproduced) | paints each, every foot control inside the foot and none overlapping another |
| The analyser: a full-scale sine | 0 dB at 1 kHz on a bin, within 1.5 dB between bins (the Hann window's 1.42 dB) |
| The analyser's tap: a writer and a reader on two threads, 16 million samples | every read a run of consecutive samples, or none |
| The response drawn against the filter | within 1e-6 dB of the impulse response's Fourier transform, every type, both forms of tan |

## For the author

- **The look.** The tilt of the analyser (3 dB per octave about 1 kHz), its fall (24 dB a second)
  and its scale (0 to −90 dB over the plot's height) are display choices, in `editor/Spectrum.h`
  and `CurveView::paintSpectrum`.
- **The hosts.** Everything above is headless or pluginval. The editor in REAPER, Live, Bitwig and
  Samplitude, touch on a touch screen, and the channel cells on a real multichannel track are
  untried: the handoff lists what to look at.
